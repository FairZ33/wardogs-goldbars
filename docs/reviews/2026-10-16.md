# Season review — October 16, 2026

Official announcements confirm Update 0.2.0 / Season 2 launched October 15.
The former Black & Gold AK offer was removed; the planner's named 30-bar preset
is therefore replaced by a generic 30-bar target. Existing numeric saved targets
remain valid, but users should select a current goal.

Eight new camos are identified in the official notes. Their bar costs are not
specified there, so no prices were invented or assigned. The planner lists their
names and directs users to a custom target based on an in-game listing.

The Gold Market unlock is listed at WARDOG level 35, $150,000. The planner notes
that this unlock cost must be reserved separately if still needed. This is not
evidence that Gold Exchange access has identical rules.

Earning multipliers and vendor discounts changed, but no gold-rate reset,
price-setting algorithm change or revised gold exchange/refund terms were
verified. The historical cutoff remains September 10; a season name alone does
not establish a price-history reset. Reassess if official evidence changes.
